## Loader Policy

- `제조사` A/J, `국내/외자` B/D, `판매사` C/H, `ATC` F/P, `성분` L/Q are duplicate semantic columns in the 24 fixed dimension area. The loader keeps the first occurrence as the canonical 19-dimension schema and drops later duplicate occurrences after recording this audit.

- `판매사2` is a distinct explicit header and is retained as canonical `판매사2`.

- This preserves the approved 19 common dimension schema. If MI later defines A/J or C/H as distinct business concepts, add explicit catalog names and reload.
